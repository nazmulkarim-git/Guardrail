How Forsig works

Pause risky agent actions. Review. Resume safely.

Forsig sits at the moment your agent needs judgment, not around every model call.

01

Risky action

Your agent is about to send, spend, update, deploy, or call an external tool.

02

Pause execution

The workflow calls Forsig and waits instead of continuing blindly.

03

Human review

A reviewer approves, rejects, edits, adds context, or takes over.

04

Safe resume

The agent receives a structured decision and every step is logged.

Why Forsig

Why not just use Slack or a custom approval queue?

Signed review links

Each approval request can be scoped, time-limited, and tied to the exact escalation.

Structured decisions

Your agent receives a machine-readable approve, reject, edit, context, or takeover response.

Fail-closed defaults

If nobody responds before timeout, risky actions should stop by default.

Audit trails

Track what the agent proposed, who decided, what changed, and when the agent resumed.
